Our story

Pure Ceylon flavour, kept intact.

The seas around Sri Lanka are warm, rich and full of life — and so is the seafood that comes from them. At Lihini Seafoods we're built around one simple idea: protect that natural flavour from the moment the catch is landed to the moment it reaches your kitchen.

That means careful handling, a cold chain that stays unbroken, and a team that gives every order the same attention — whether it's a single box for a restaurant or a larger load for a wholesaler.
